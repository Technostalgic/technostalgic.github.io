About Me

Hi, I'm Isaiah. I like cats, astronomy, video games, and minimalist backpacking/camping.
💙 🐈‍⬛ 🪐

I have always had a passion for software development, it's sad to see that AI is coming in and taking that away from so many people. I, personally, find it to be a fascinating time to be alive. While I'm sad that it does seem like my work in the future will have less to do with actual coding than traditional software development roles, I'm excited to see where the world takes us. I'm excited to see what solutions we will come up with for the many problems that generative AI brings along with it, and prove ourselves as a resilient and resourceful species as we have many times in the past.
